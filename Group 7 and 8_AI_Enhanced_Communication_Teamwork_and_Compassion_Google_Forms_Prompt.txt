Questionnaire Title

Theoretical Framework

• Madeleine Leininger - Culture Care Diversity and Universality Theory

Questionnaire Structure

Generate exactly 15 questionnaire items organized into the following
subscales.

Part A. Using AI to Analyze Assessment Data and Generate Clinical
Insights (Rogers) - 3 items

Part B. Using AI to Improve Team Communication (Benner) - 4 items

Part C. Using AI to Communicate with Compassion (Watson) - 4 items

Part D. Using AI to Plan Culturally Sensitive Nursing Care (Leininger) -
4 items

Generate original, concise, unbiased, and academically appropriate
statements. Each item must measure only one construct and should avoid
leading, double-barreled, or ambiguous wording.

The questionnaire should be appropriate for undergraduate and graduate
nursing students and focus on the responsible use of artificial
intelligence in nursing education and clinical practice.

1 = Strongly disagree 2 = Disagree 3 = Somewhat disagree 4 = Neither
agree nor disagree 5 = Somewhat agree 6 = Agree 7 = Strongly agree
