Questionnaire Title

Nursing Students’ Distress and Burnout Questionnaire

Theoretical Framework

Develop the questionnaire according to the following nursing theories:

• Florence Nightingale - Environmental Theory • Virginia Henderson -
Need Theory • Nola J. Pender - Health Promotion Model • Jean Watson -
Theory of Human Caring • Madeleine Leininger - Culture Care Diversity
and Universality Theory

Questionnaire Structure

Generate exactly 15 questionnaire items organized into the following
subscales.

Part A. Environment and Safety (Nightingale) - 3 items

Part B. Basic Needs and Functional Wellness (Henderson) - 3 items

Part C. Health Promotion Habits (Pender) - 3 items

Part D. Expressions of Caring and Compassion for Others (Watson) - 3
items

Part E. Peer Support (Leininger) - 3 items

Generate original, concise, unbiased, and academically appropriate
statements. Each item must measure only one construct and should avoid
leading, double-barreled, or ambiguous wording.

Response Scale

Use a 7-point Likert scale for every questionnaire item.

1 = Strongly disagree 2 = Disagree 3 = Somewhat disagree 4 = Neither
agree nor disagree 5 = Somewhat agree 6 = Agree 7 = Strongly agree

Scoring

-   Total questionnaire items: 15
-   Possible total score range: 15 to 105

Interpretation Guide

Use the following interpretation exactly.

• High = 83 to 105 • Moderate = 53 to 82 • Low = 15 to 52

Conceptual Framework

Independent Variable (IV): Peer Support (Leininger)

Mediator: Health Promotion Habits (Pender)

Moderator: Expressions of Caring and Compassion for Others (Watson)

Dependent Variable (DV): Nursing Students’ Distress and Burnout

Confounder: Average Hours of Sleep Per Night

Covariate: Year Level

Ensure exactly one IV, one DV, one mediator, one moderator, one
confounder, and one covariate.
